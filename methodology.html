FRAMEWORKS & METHODOLOGY

Methodology

Two tracks, two logics: on the Red Team side, you progress through phases up to the compromise objective agreed with the client. On the Blue Team side, you start from a signal and work backwards to the root cause to reduce detection time.

RED TEAM

Penetration test workflow

Inspired by PTES, the OWASP Testing Guide, and NIST SP 800-115.

  1. ReconnaissancePassive and active information gathering on the target.
  2. EnumerationIdentifying services, technologies and attack surfaces.
  3. Vulnerability researchManual and tool-assisted analysis of the scope.
  4. ExploitationControlled validation of identified vulnerabilities.
  5. Post-exploitationAssessing real impact: pivoting, privilege escalation.
  6. Risk analysisScoring each finding using CVSS 3.1 / 4.0.
  7. ReportingExecutive summary and prioritized recommendations.
BLUE TEAM

Incident response cycle

Inspired by NIST SP 800-61 and mapped against the MITRE ATT&CK matrix.

  1. PreparationAvailable log sources, detection hypotheses set ahead of time.
  2. Detection & analysisAlert triage, correlation, signal confirmation.
  3. QualificationMapping to one or more MITRE ATT&CK techniques.
  4. ContainmentLimiting scope without losing evidence.
  5. Eradication & recoveryRemoving the root cause, returning to a healthy state.
  6. Lessons learnedFinal timeline, measured detection delay, detection rule written.

Rating matrix — Red Team

Severity based on the CVSS 3.1 / 4.0 score, with a recommended remediation timeline.
